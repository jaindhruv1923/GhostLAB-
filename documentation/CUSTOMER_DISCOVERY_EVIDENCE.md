## 1. Executive Summary & Fieldwork Methodology

Pursuant to **Module 2: Opportunity Identification and Problem Discovery** of the AIE-701 course syllabus, this dossier documents qualitative and semi-structured fieldwork conducted across the Delhi-NCR academic innovation corridor (BMU Propel Incubator, DTU, and IIT Delhi research clusters). 

To avoid the common pitfall of building in a vacuum, the problem statement was subjected to empirical testing across four critical stakeholder personas:
1. **Student Hardware Maker / Capstone Founder:** Evaluates the demand-side pain of patent intimidation, component pricing, and redesign trial-and-error.
2. **Graduate Doctoral Scholar / Inventor:** Evaluates the supply-side tragedy of grant termination, thesis abandonment, and zero commercial royalty participation.
3. **Campus Incubation / TTO Manager:** Evaluates the institutional bottleneck of patent maintenance fees, administrative backlogs, and lack of commercialization staff.
4. **Intellectual Property & Bar Council Legal Counsel:** Evaluates statutory compliance under Section 3 of the Advocates Act, 1961 and Section 53 of the Indian Patents Act, 1970.

## 2. Structured Stakeholder Interview Transcripts

### Interview 01: The Student Hardware Founder
- **Interviewee:** Rohan S. (Final Year B.Tech Mechatronics, Capstone Team Lead)
- **Setting:** BMU Propel Incubator & Maker Workshop
- **Date & Duration:** 28 September 2026 • 42 Minutes
- **Context:** Building an autonomous solar robotic lawn weed-cutter for agricultural capstone.

#### Verbatim Transcript Excerpt:
> **Dhruv (Interviewer):** *Rohan, when your team started designing the rotary blade gimbal, did you check existing Indian or US patents?*  
> **Rohan:** *"Honestly? We were terrified to look. Our faculty mentor told us that if we read patents, we could get hit with 'willful infringement' damages. But even if we wanted to do a Freedom-to-Operate (FTO) search, I called an IP law firm in Connaught Place — they quoted ₹1.2 Lakhs plus GST just to read the claims! That is more than our entire team’s seed budget for 3D printing and PCBs. So we just closed our eyes, ordered generic stepper motors from Nehru Place, and hoped nobody sues us."*  
> 
> **Dhruv:** *If a platform gave you verified CAD files of an expired 2004 IIT Bombay agriculture patent with an automated non-infringing engineering workaround and an official campus safe-harbor deed for ₹1,999, would your team use it?*  
> **Rohan:** *"Tomorrow morning. ₹1,999 is less than what we burned last week on two melted stepper driver boards. Having a signed legal safe-harbor that says our geometry doesn't infringe Claim 1 would let us sleep at night."*

#### Core Discoveries:
- **Pain Point:** Legal intimidation & prohibitive attorney retainers (₹1.2L–₹1.5L).
- **Behavior:** "Blind building" — students intentionally avoid prior art out of fear.
- **Willingness to Pay:** High elasticity up to ₹3,500; ₹1,999 is perceived as an instant "no-brainer" student expenditure.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis: The "5 Whys" of Campus Science Dormancy

1. **Why does 95% of funded university research sit abandoned on lab shelves?**  
   *Because student researchers leave the university once their thesis is completed.*
2. **Why don't subsequent student founders pick up and commercialize this existing research?**  
   *Because junior students cannot access the raw CAD files, firmware, and test data, and are terrified of patent infringement.*
3. **Why can't students simply license the technology through the university TTO?**  
   *Because fewer than 5% of Indian universities have full-time commercial TTO staff, and existing transfer agreements require 12 to 18 months of committee approvals.*
4. **Why don't student founders conduct an independent patent search and design around it?**  
   *Because professional patent databases (PatSnap, Clarivate) cost ₹10 Lakhs/year, and patent law firms demand ₹1.5 Lakh upfront retainers for claim analysis.*
5. **ROOT CAUSE:**  
   *Extreme information asymmetry and legal cost barriers separate abandoned, grant-funded campus CAD assets from young hardware builders. An automated, affordable AI engineering atelier that deconstructs claims and issues 1-click safe-harbor deeds solves the systemic deadlock.*
