## 2. Core Vision & Synthesis (Direction A + Direction C)

In modern higher education and national research centers (IITs, BMU, DTU, CSIR, BIRAC), two systemic breakdowns occur simultaneously:

1. **The Academic Graveyard (Direction A):** Over 95% of funded master's and doctoral inventions sit abandoned once grant funding concludes. Professors publish and move on; university Technology Transfer Offices (TTOs) lack commercial sales staff.
2. **The Patent Gatekeeping Wall (Direction C):** Student founders who wish to build physical hardware or DeepTech are either blocked by expensive law firm retainers (&#x20B9;1.5 Lakhs) or remain oblivious to 1.8M+ legendary patents that have already expired into the public domain.

**GhostLab synthesizes both domains into an eye-soothing, dignified salon:**
- It rescues abandoned university lab prototypes (CAD assemblies, firmware, test datasets) for a flat &#x20B9;1,999 student license.
- It channels **80% of every license fee directly to the original student researcher's fellowship**, providing real second chances.
- It provides an automated **Public Domain & Generative Design-Around Co-Pilot** that steps outside active corporate patent claims using the All-Elements Rule.

## 3. Key Modules & Application Architecture

### View 1: The Invention Archive & Dormant Vault (`#archive`)

- Live telemetry: Sunk grant values (e.g., &#x20B9;14L to &#x20B9;65L), year shelved, researcher origin, and instant autopsy triggers.

### View 2: Deep Invention Autopsy & Rebirth Dossier (`#autopsy`)
- **The Research Autopsy:** Transparent diagnosis of why the science was abandoned (*lead researcher graduation, domestic PCB minimums, regulatory hurdle*).
- **The 2026 Commercial Rebirth Plan:** How a student venture pairs 2022 mechanical design with 2026 edge AI chips (ESP32-S3, Hailo-8) to ship a ₹10 Cr company.
- **Granular Asset Stripper Checklist:** Toggle individual files (Kinematic CAD, FreeRTOS firmware, KiCad netlists, 42-hour IMU spectrograms) with live total calculations.

### View 3: Public Domain & Design-Around Co-Pilot (`#copilot`)
- Multimodal CAD boundary ingest (`.step`, `.stl`, `.kicad_pcb`).
- 360-degree spatial vector radar scope scanning 1.2M patents using 1536-D cosine embeddings.
- **All-Elements Claim Deconstructor:** Flags fatal independent claim bottlenecks (e.g., US 10,482,911 Claim 1 Element C).
- **3 Generative Engineering Workarounds:**
  - *Workaround 01 (Mechanical):* Single-axis eccentric flexure cam (3D-printable nylon).
