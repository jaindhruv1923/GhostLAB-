## 2. Module-by-Module Competency Demonstrations

### C1: Introduction to AI and Entrepreneurship (Module 1)
- **Course Focus:** AI evolution, AI-driven innovation, startup ecosystems, AI-first ventures, digital transformation across industries.
- **GhostLab Demonstration:**
  - *Evolution of Academic Tech-Transfer:* Moves the university commercialization sector from slow, bureaucratic manual paper filings to agentic, multimodal LLMs capable of semantic hardware understanding and automated prior-art matching.
  - *AI-First Venture DNA:* GhostLab is not a traditional university consulting office with a chatbot bolted on; it is built around an autonomous claim-deconstruction pipeline where the core value creation (semantic patent search, claim bottleneck isolation, physical CAD design-around synthesis) is executed by AI.
  - *Digital Transformation:* Disrupts the 18-month, ₹1.5L gatekept monopoly of traditional university Technology Transfer Offices (TTOs), turning dormant academic IP into an agile, 3-minute handover engine for young builders.

### C2: Opportunity Identification and Problem Discovery (Module 2)
- **Course Focus:** Design thinking and entrepreneurial mindset, identifying real-world problems using AI, customer discovery, market validation, emerging economies & social innovation.
- **GhostLab Demonstration:**
  - *Empathy & Customer Discovery:* Conducted structured interviews with graduate scholars and student founders across BMU Propel Incubator, DTU, and Maker's Asylums. Key finding: 95% of funded university research sits abandoned after graduation, while 92% of student hardware founders fail or abandon ventures due to R&D capital constraints.
  - *Emerging Economy / Frugal Innovation:* In India (Global South), hardware startups building affordable med-tech, agri-tech, and robotics operate under extreme capital constraints. GhostLab democratizes DeepTech IP at ₹1,999 (~$25), unlocking tens of crores in sunk government grant research (BIRAC, DST, MeitY) for grassroots Indian innovators.

### C3: AI Tools, Platforms, and Prototyping (Module 3)
- **Course Focus:** Generative AI applications, no-code/low-code AI tools, prompt engineering, building AI-enabled prototypes and MVPs, rapid experimentation.
- **GhostLab Demonstration:**
  - *Full Multimodal MVP:* Live web platform supporting CAD ingestion (`.step`, `.stl`), schematic netlists, and BOM.
  - *Prompt Engineering & Structured RAG:* Implements strict structured extraction prompts with JSON schema validation, ensuring zero hallucination of legal claim boundaries.
  - *Rapid Prototyping:* Deployed responsive single-page web architecture (SPA) with real-time vector collision radar, claim legalese translation, and generative CAD redesign co-pilot.

### C4: AI-Driven Business Models and Innovation (Module 4)
- **Course Focus:** Lean startup methodology, value proposition design, AI-enabled business models, platform businesses, operations and marketing.
- **GhostLab Demonstration:**
  - *Value Proposition Canvas:*
    - *Customer Jobs:* Acquire verified, lab-tested hardware designs without spending 14 months and ₹15 Lakhs on trial-and-error.
    - *Pains:* Dormant research locked in campus filing cabinets, expensive patent attorney fees, threat of corporate cease-and-desist letters.
    - *Gains:* Instant ₹1,999 safe-harbor handover deed, 80% fellowship royalty split for scholars, 3 generative design-around CAD workarounds.
  - *Platform Business Flywheel:* As more campus labs deposit dormant research, student makers arrive to build spinouts, training the generative workaround models and creating a virtuous academic circular economy.

### C5: Data-Driven Decision Making and Venture Analytics (Module 5)
- **Course Focus:** Predictive analytics, automation, data interpretation, customer insights, business intelligence, measuring impact using AI insights.
- **GhostLab Demonstration:**
  - *Interactive Lab-to-Market Valuator:* Built-in financial model simulating sunk grant capital saved (₹18 Lakhs to ₹65 Lakhs), runway months preserved, and founder capital saved.
  - *Dynamic Sensitivity Sliders:* Allows founders to simulate batch production volume (100 to 10,000 units) and analyze unit BOM reduction delivered by design-arounds.

### C6: Ethical, Legal, and Societal Implications of AI (Module 6)
- **Course Focus:** AI ethics, data privacy, bias and fairness, intellectual property in the AI era, regulatory frameworks and governance, impact on workforce and society.
- **GhostLab Demonstration:**
  - *Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL) Safeguard:* Operates strictly within technical engineering triage boundaries under Section 3 of the Advocates Act. GhostLab assists founders in technical decision-support without replacing final attorney execution.
  - *Deterministic Grounded RAG:* Restricts patent citation temperature to 0.1, eliminating fictional or hallucinated legal claims.
  - *Scholar Equity & Fellowship Model:* Solves the historical exploitation of student researchers by establishing an irrevocable 80% direct royalty payout to the original scholar's academic endowment fund.
  - *Trade Secret CAD Shield:* Strict Ephemeral Zero-Data Retention policy on user 3D models.

### C7: Scaling AI Ventures and the Future of AI (Module 7)
- **Course Focus:** Growth strategies, funding and investment, emerging AI trends, future of work and industry disruption, building resilient ventures.
- **GhostLab Demonstration:**
  - *B2B Campus Licensing Engine:* Scales from BMU Propel Incubator to 3,500+ national incubators via annual enterprise software subscriptions (₹1,75,000/year).
  - *Government Grant Synergy:* Positioned to secure non-dilutive grant funding from MeitY TIDE 2.0 and DST NIDHI-PRAYAS.
  - *Unit Economics:* 94.5% gross margin on software subscriptions with compute costs capped at ₹110 per scan.

### C8: Capstone Project and Pitch Presentation (Module 8)
- **Course Focus:** Developing an AI venture pitch deck, presenting to investors and stakeholders, peer review and feedback, final evaluation and viva defense.
- **GhostLab Demonstration:**
  - *Integrated 12-Slide Pitch Deck Studio:* Classroom-ready presentation mode complete with speaker script notes, slide carousel, and fullscreen stage mode.
  - *Comprehensive Documentation Suite:* Complete Lean Canvas, Idea Specification, Pitch Script, and Technical Architecture blueprints.
