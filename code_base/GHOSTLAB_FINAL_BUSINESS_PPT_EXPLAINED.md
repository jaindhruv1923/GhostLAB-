#### The Formula & Substitution Boxes (Slide 13 Layout)
- **Breakeven Sales Level Formula Box:**
  $$\text{Breakeven Sales Level} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{1 - \text{Variable Costs Percentage}}$$
- **Substituting Values Box:**
  $$\text{Breakeven Sales Level} = \frac{43,500}{1 - 0.50} = \frac{43,500}{0.50} = \mathbf{87,000}$$
  *(In Indian Rupees: $\frac{\text{₹}36,10,500}{0.50} = \mathbf{\text{₹}72,21,000}$)*
- **Unit Break-Even Volume Box:**
  $$\text{BEP (Units)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{Price} - \text{VC per unit}} = \frac{\$43,500}{\$80 - \$40} = \mathbf{1,088 \text{ Licenses}}$$
- **Interpretation Box:**
  - *To break even, we need sales of $87,000 annually.*
  - *This calculation assumes variable costs are 50% of our sales revenue and fixed costs are as mentioned ($43,500).*
  - *Unit Volume Feasibility: 1,088 licenses across 15 incubators requires only ~72 student licenses per campus/year (under 6 per month), capturing only 2.42% of graduating engineering capstone students.*
  - *47.9% Margin of Safety provides robust protection against early market adoption volatility.*

  2. $VC\% = 50\%$ gives a contribution margin ratio of $1 - 0.50 = 0.50$.
  3. Dividing $FC$ by $0.50$ doubles the required sales: $43,500 \div 0.50 = \$87,000$.
