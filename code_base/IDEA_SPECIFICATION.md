## 1. Executive Summary

Every year, universities and national research institutions across India and the globe spend billions of dollars in government and institutional grants (DST, BIRAC, MeitY, ICMR, CSIR, NSF). Brilliant graduate scholars and professors develop working prototypes, injection mold CADs, PCB netlists, and novel algorithms. Yet, **over 95% of these funded inventions sit permanently abandoned** in university file cabinets and unindexed PDF gazettes once grant cycles conclude or students graduate.

Concurrently, early-stage student hardware and DeepTech founders spend an average of 14 months and ₹15–25 Lakhs attempting to build complex mechanical mechanisms from scratch, frequently receiving devastating cease-and-desist notices or abandoning ventures when custom tooling costs exceed pre-seed budgets. Furthermore, over 1.8 million valuable patents have expired into the public domain, yet early-stage founders lack the tools to verify their legal clearance.

**GhostLab** solves this double tragedy by creating an **eye-soothing, dignified academic technology transfer and patent rebirth atelier**. It rescues dormant university research and public domain patent blueprints for a flat ₹1,999 student license, channeling **80% of all micro-license revenues directly to the original researcher's fellowship fund**. For inventions with active prior-art overlap, GhostLab's **Generative Design-Around Engine** automatically synthesizes 3 tangible physical engineering workarounds to step outside legal claim boundaries under the All-Elements Rule.

## 2. The Core Problem Statement

### 2.1 The Academic Filing Cabinet (Supply-Side Breakdown)
1. **Misaligned Academic Incentives:** Professors are evaluated on publication count, citations, and securing the next research grant. Commercializing a working lab prototype requires sales, distribution, and corporate outreach &mdash; skills outside traditional faculty responsibilities.
2. **Understaffed Technology Transfer Offices:** Less than 5% of Indian higher education institutions have dedicated, full-time Technology Transfer Offices (TTOs). The few that exist focus exclusively on multi-crore pharmaceutical licensing, ignoring physical hardware, robotics, and agricultural prototypes.
3. **Graduate Scholar Brain Drain:** When master's and doctoral scholars graduate, their custom CAD models and test code remain on campus hard drives without documentation or maintenance.

### 2.2 The Student Founder Barrier (Demand-Side Breakdown)
1. **The ₹1.5L Legal Wall:** Traditional IP law firms charge ₹1,50,000 to ₹3,50,000 for a single Freedom-to-Operate search. Student startups operate on shoestring budgets and gamble on ignorance.
2. **14 Months of Sunk R&D:** Building custom camera gimbals, optical pulse rings, or robotic weeding linkages requires months of mechanical trial-and-error, draining finite pre-seed runway.
3. **The Unhelpful Legal "No":** Attorneys issue binary rejections without engineering redesigns. Founders are told what they cannot build, but never how to redesign around the claim.
