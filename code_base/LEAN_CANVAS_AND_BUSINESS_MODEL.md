## 2. Value Proposition Canvas (VPC)

### 2.1 The Student Hardware Founder Profile
- **Customer Jobs:**
  - Launch a functional physical hardware or DeepTech product before graduation.
  - Avoid patent infringement lawsuits and cease-and-desist letters from multinational corporations.
  - Minimize prototype trial-and-error costs on a pre-seed budget.
- **Customer Pains:**
  - Spending 14 months and ₹15 Lakhs reinventing mechanical mechanisms from scratch.
  - Facing ₹1,50,000 legal retainer bills from traditional IP law firms.
  - Bureaucratic 18-month university technology transfer processes demanding 15% equity.
- **Customer Gains:**
  - Instant ₹1,999 1-click access to verified, grant-funded campus CAD assemblies and firmware.
  - Automatic synthesis of 3 tangible physical workarounds to escape active corporate claims.
  - 100% legal clearance with 0% equity dilution for student founders.

### 2.2 The Research Scholar Profile
- **Customer Jobs:**
  - See their master's or doctoral thesis create real-world societal impact.
  - Secure research funding and industry recognition for their engineering inventions.
- **Customer Pains:**
  - Watching their working lab prototype gather dust on campus shelves after grant funding ends.
  - Receiving zero financial compensation from university filing cabinets.
- **Customer Gains:**
  - Direct 80% fellowship royalty payout (₹1,599 per license) sent to their ongoing research fund.
  - Public academic recognition as the inventor of a commercialized hardware spinout.

## 5. Expansion Pack Lean Experiment Validation Framework (Features 21 to 40)

Under Module 4 (Lean Startup & Business Model Innovation), GhostLab validates its business model hypotheses through structured empirical sprint cycles:

### 5.1 Build-Measure-Learn Validation Ledger (Feature 31)
- **Hypothesis 1 (Customer Demand):** "Commercial drone operators in Haryana prefer an open-CAD gimbal kit at INR 4,800 over proprietary DJI replacement parts at INR 45,000."
  - *Method:* Customer Discovery Interview (n=32).
  - *Empirical Result:* 75.0% validated (24 signed LOIs, $p=0.008$).
  - *Decision:* **Persevere** &mdash; proceed to rapid PCB prototype assembly.
- **Hypothesis 2 (Market Willingness-to-Pay):** "Smallholder tomato farmers will purchase an unsubsidized rotary weeder at INR 8,500."
  - *Method:* Price Sensitivity Van Westendorp Test (n=40).
  - *Empirical Result:* Only 22% willing at INR 8,500; 78% demand a rental-hub or INR 5,200 price point.
  - *Decision:* **Pivot** &mdash; pivoted from hardware sales to FPO equipment hiring centre subscription model.
- **Hypothesis 3 (TTO Resistance Pre-Mortem):** "University technology transfer committees will stall startup prototypes for over 6 months."
  - *Method:* Venture Pre-Mortem Simulation (Feature 29) + Interviews with 10 TTO officers.
  - *Empirical Result:* 62% of historical public university spin-outs experienced administrative stall.
  - *Decision:* **Mitigate** &mdash; built 1-Click pre-approved Safe-Harbor assignment deed with pre-set 80/20 royalty split.
